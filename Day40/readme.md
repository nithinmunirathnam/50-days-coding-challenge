## 📌 Overview
This project focuses on analyzing production, defect rates, sales performance, and profitability in a packaging company using SQL. The company manufactures packaging products such as boxes, bottles, and wrappers, and the analysis helps identify production efficiency, revenue trends, defect issues, and inventory insights.

The project uses SQL queries to perform data analysis on production and sales datasets stored in relational database tables.

---

## 🎯 Objective
To practice SQL concepts by solving real-world business problems related to the packaging industry using:

- SQL Queries
- Joins
- Aggregate Functions
- GROUP BY
- HAVING Clause
- Window Functions
- Profit and Revenue Analysis
- Business Insights

---

## ❓ Questions

### 🔹 Task 1: Defect Rate per Product
Calculate defect rate using:

Defect Rate = Defective Quantity / Quantity Produced

Display:
- Product Name
- Total Produced
- Total Defective
- Defect Rate

### 🔹 Task 2: Top Performing Product (Revenue)
Find the product with the highest total revenue.

Revenue Formula:

Revenue = Quantity Sold × Selling Price per Unit

### 🔹 Task 3: Monthly Production Trend
Display:
- Month
- Total Production

Sort the results month-wise.

### 🔹 Task 4: Category-wise Profit
Calculate:
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Total Profit

Profit Formula:

Profit = Selling Price − Cost Price

Group the analysis category-wise.

### 🔹 Task 5: Identify High Defect Days
Find production days where:

Defect Rate > 5%

Display:
- Production Date
- Product Name
- Defect Rate

### 🔹 Task 6: Running Total Revenue
Calculate cumulative revenue over time using SQL Window Functions.

### 🔹 Task 7: Best Category by Profit Margin
Calculate profit margin using:

Profit Margin = Profit / Revenue

Identify the category with the highest profit margin.

### 🔹 Task 8: Unsold Inventory
Find products where:

Produced Quantity > Sold Quantity

Display:
- Product Name
- Total Produced
- Total Sold
- Unsold Inventory
